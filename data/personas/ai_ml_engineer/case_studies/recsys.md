# Product recommendations

## Context

When I started with Signal Labs, a bookstore business, the homepage showed the same bestsellers to everyone.

## What I did

- Implicit-feedback matrix factorisation.
- Re-ranked for stock and margin.
- A/B tested against bestsellers.
- Deployed on their existing cloud account with least-privilege roles.
- Pulled the history straight from their warehouse with window-function queries.

## Results

- Click-through up 27%.
- Revenue per session up 5%.
- 22k SKUs.

Engagement: 11 weeks, finished 2023-06.

## Stack

Python, Recommendation Systems, scikit-learn
