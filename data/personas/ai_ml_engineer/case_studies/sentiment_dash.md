# Review sentiment monitoring

## Context

A hospitality client, Lumen Health, came to me because managers read reviews by hand and missed trends.

## What I did

- Aspect extraction for food, service, price and cleanliness.
- Weekly sentiment trend dashboard.
- Started from an open pretrained transformer checkpoint.
- Pulled the history straight from their warehouse with window-function queries.
- Alerts when an aspect drops.

## Results

- 54k reviews analysed.
- Aspect accuracy 89%.
- Issues spotted 2 weeks earlier.

Engagement: 12 weeks, finished 2024-04.

## Stack

Python, NLP, Data Visualization
