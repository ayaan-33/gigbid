# Data labelling workflow

## Context

Orchid Health (research lab) had a familiar problem: the team had 200k unlabelled documents and two annotators.

## What I did

- Labelling functions for weak supervision.
- Covered the critical paths with automated tests before refactoring.
- Inter-annotator agreement checks.
- Did the feature work in dataframes and kept every transformation in versioned notebooks.
- Active learning loop.

## Results

- Labelled 81k documents.
- Agreement kappa 0.75.
- Labelling cost down 58%.
