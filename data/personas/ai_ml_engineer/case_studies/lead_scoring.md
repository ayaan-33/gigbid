# B2B lead scoring

## Context

When I started with Lumen Health, a SaaS business, sales reps worked leads in arrival order.

## What I did

- Features from CRM activity and firmographics.
- Calibrated logistic regression the team could read.
- Did the feature work in dataframes and kept every transformation in versioned notebooks.
- Scores pushed back into the CRM nightly.
- Pulled the history straight from their warehouse with window-function queries.

## Results

- Conversion in the top quintile 4x baseline.
- Pipeline up 18% in a quarter.
- 22k leads scored.

Engagement: 11 weeks, finished 2025-03.

## Stack

Python, scikit-learn, Statistics
