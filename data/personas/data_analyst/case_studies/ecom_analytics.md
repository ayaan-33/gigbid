# Shopify store analytics

## Context

A skincare brand client, Granite Labs, came to me because the founder checked five apps to understand one day of sales.

## What I did

- Combined Shopify, GA4 and ad spend data.
- Basket analysis for bundles.
- Reported confidence intervals rather than single numbers.
- Left them with a workbook the finance team could still open and audit.
- One daily dashboard.

## Results

- Average order value up 6% from bundles.
- 4 data sources unified.
- Daily review time down to 7 minutes.

Engagement: 13 weeks, finished 2025-11.

## Stack

SQL, Google Analytics, Looker
