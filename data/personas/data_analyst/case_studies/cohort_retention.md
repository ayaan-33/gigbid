# Cohort retention analysis

## Context

Beacon Partners (subscription box) had a familiar problem: leadership debated churn without agreeing on numbers.

## What I did

- Defined cohorts and churn with finance.
- Designed the charts so a manager can read them in ten seconds.
- Survival curves by acquisition channel.
- Scripted the repetitive steps.
- LTV by cohort with confidence intervals.

## Results

- Identified a 15% higher-churn onboarding segment.
- LTV estimates within 7% of actuals.
- 31 monthly cohorts.

Engagement: 3 weeks, finished 2022-08.
