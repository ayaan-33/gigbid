# Customer survey analysis

## Context

A consumer electronics client, Granite Studio, came to me because survey results sat in raw exports nobody read.

## What I did

- Cleaned and weighted responses.
- Did the feature work in dataframes and kept every transformation in versioned notebooks.
- Driver analysis for NPS.
- Themes from open-text answers.
- Checked the statistical models in a second toolkit.

## Results

- 15k responses analysed.
- 3 key drivers identified.
- NPS up 14 points after fixes.

Engagement: 8 weeks, finished 2023-09.

Excel, Statistics, Data Visualization
