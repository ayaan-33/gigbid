# Operations KPI dashboard

## Context

A call centre client, Copper Co., came to me because shift leads could not see backlog until it was too late.

## What I did

- Hourly refresh from the WMS database.
- Built the extract-clean-load steps so the numbers refresh without anyone touching them.
- Left them with a workbook the finance team could still open and audit.
- Traffic-light KPIs per shift.
- Drill-down to order level.

## Results

- Late orders down 17%.
- 5 sites on one dashboard.
- Refresh every 49 minutes.

Engagement: 9 weeks, finished 2023-04.

## Stack

Tableau, SQL, Data Visualization
