# Data cleaning pipeline in Python

## Context

A research group client, Willow Partners, came to me because analysts spent most of their week fixing messy exports.

## What I did

- Pandas pipeline with schema validation.
- Deduplication and fuzzy matching of names.
- Quality report emailed after each run.
- Pulled the history straight from their warehouse with window-function queries.
- Covered the critical paths with automated tests before refactoring.

## Results

- Cleaning time down 81%.
- 1 million rows per run.
- Duplicate records down 88%.

Engagement: 12 weeks, finished 2024-08.

Python, pandas, ETL
