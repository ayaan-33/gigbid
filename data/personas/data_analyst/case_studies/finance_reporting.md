# Management accounts reporting

## Context

When I started with Signal Group, a clinic network business, owners got the P&L six weeks after month end.

## What I did

- Connected accounting exports to a model.
- Budget vs actual variance views.
- Built the extract-clean-load steps so the numbers refresh without anyone touching them.
- Cash runway forecast.
- Designed the charts so a manager can read them in ten seconds.

## Results

- Reporting lag from 5 weeks to 3 days.
- 19 entities consolidated.
- Variance flags caught 5 billing errors.

Engagement: 13 weeks, finished 2024-06.

Excel, Power BI, SQL
