# Marketing channel attribution

## Context

A DTC brand client, Pine Analytics, came to me because marketing could not tell which channels drove sales.

## What I did

- GA4 export to BigQuery.
- Position-based attribution compared with last-click.
- Designed the charts so a manager can read them in ten seconds.
- Looker Studio report for weekly reviews.
- Built the extract-clean-load steps so the numbers refresh without anyone touching them.

## Results

- Budget shifted 22% to better channels.
- Cost per acquisition down 13%.
- 9 channels tracked.

Engagement: 4 weeks, finished 2022-01.
