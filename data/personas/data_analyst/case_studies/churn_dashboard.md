# Customer churn dashboard

## Context

A SaaS client, Orchid Systems, came to me because customer success learned about churn after it happened.

## What I did

- Health score from usage and tickets.
- Weekly at-risk list.
- Trend views by plan and region.
- Left them with a workbook the finance team could still open and audit.
- Scripted the repetitive steps.

## Results

- At-risk accounts flagged 3 weeks earlier.
- Retention up 3 points.
- 8k accounts scored.

Engagement: 3 weeks, finished 2026-04.

Power BI, SQL, Statistics
