# Excel reporting automation

## Context

Signal Group (nonprofit) had a familiar problem: a finance assistant spent two days a month copying data between workbooks.

## What I did

- Power Query to pull and clean source files.
- Built the extract-clean-load steps so the numbers refresh without anyone touching them.
- Pivot-based summary with slicers.
- Pulled the history straight from their warehouse with window-function queries.
- Documented steps so anyone could refresh it.

## Results

- Monthly close prep from 16 hours to under 2.
- Zero copy-paste errors in 8 months.
- 11 source files consolidated.

Engagement: 3 weeks, finished 2022-07.
