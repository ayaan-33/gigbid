# Location and territory analysis

## Context

When I started with Signal Labs, a gym franchise business, new sites were chosen on gut feel.

## What I did

- Catchment areas from drive times.
- Designed the charts so a manager can read them in ten seconds.
- Demographic and competitor overlays.
- Scoring model for candidate sites.
- Scripted the repetitive steps.

## Results

- 25 candidate sites scored.
- New-site revenue 12% above prior openings.
- 5 regions mapped.

Engagement: 5 weeks, finished 2024-12.
