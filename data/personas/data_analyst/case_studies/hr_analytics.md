# HR attrition analytics

## Context

A retail chain client, Juniper Labs, came to me because attrition was high and nobody knew where.

## What I did

- Joined HRIS, payroll and survey data.
- Attrition drivers with logistic regression.
- Designed the charts so a manager can read them in ten seconds.
- Pulled the history straight from their warehouse with window-function queries.
- Manager-level dashboard with privacy thresholds.

## Results

- Attrition down 5 points in a year.
- 4k employees covered.
- 3 drivers acted on.

Engagement: 6 weeks, finished 2025-07.
