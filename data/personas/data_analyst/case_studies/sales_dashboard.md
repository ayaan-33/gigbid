# Sales performance dashboard

## Context

A auto parts client, Harbor Logistics, came to me because managers waited a week for a manual Excel sales pack.

## What I did

- Modelled orders, targets and territories in a star schema.
- DAX measures for growth vs target.
- Row-level security per region.
- Built the extract-clean-load steps so the numbers refresh without anyone touching them.
- Reported confidence intervals rather than single numbers.

## Results

- Reporting time from 4 days to same-day.
- 56 managers using it weekly.
- 5 spreadsheets retired.

Engagement: 14 weeks, finished 2021-11.

## Stack

Power BI, SQL, Excel, Data Visualization
