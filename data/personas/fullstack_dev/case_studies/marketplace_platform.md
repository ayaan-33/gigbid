# Two-sided marketplace platform

## Context

When I started with Signal Studio, a event staffing business, matching providers and customers by phone did not scale.

## What I did

- Listings, search and booking.
- Pushed updates to the browser over a persistent connection.
- Kept the whole codebase strictly typed.
- Stripe Connect payouts.
- Reviews and dispute flow.

## Results

- 4k completed bookings.
- Take rate automated at 12%.
- Time to match down 59%.

Engagement: 3 weeks, finished 2025-07.

## Stack

Next.js, Node.js, Stripe, PostgreSQL
