# Headless e-commerce store

## Context

When I started with Tidal Partners, a coffee roaster business, the theme store was slow and hard to customise.

## What I did

- Headless storefront on the Storefront API.
- Image optimisation and edge caching.
- Hand-built semantic, responsive markup.
- Custom bundle builder with Stripe checkout.
- Kept the whole codebase strictly typed.

## Results

- Lighthouse performance 94.
- Conversion up 23%.
- Mobile bounce rate down 19%.

Engagement: 13 weeks, finished 2020-03.

Next.js, Shopify, React, Stripe
