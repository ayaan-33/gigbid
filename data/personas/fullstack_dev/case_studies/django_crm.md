# Custom CRM in Django

## Context

A recruiting agency client, Pine Logistics, came to me because the sales team ran deals in shared spreadsheets.

## What I did

- Django models for accounts, deals and activities.
- Background jobs for email sync.
- Moved slow work to a background job queue.
- Role-based permissions.
- Shipped it as a container image so their ops team could run it anywhere.

## Results

- 79 daily users.
- Deal cycle visibility for 3 teams.
- Spreadsheet work cut 8 hours a week.

Engagement: 4 weeks, finished 2021-06.

## Stack

Django, PostgreSQL, REST API
