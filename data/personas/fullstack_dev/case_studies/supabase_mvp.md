# MVP on Supabase

## Context

A community app client, Bright Group, came to me because founders needed a working product for investor demos in weeks.

## What I did

- Supabase auth, storage and row-level security.
- React frontend with optimistic updates.
- Weekly demo cadence.
- Built a small utility-first design system.
- Kept everything in their existing relational database with proper migrations.

## Results

- Shipped in 5 weeks.
- 1204 beta users.
- Raised a pre-seed round after demos.

Engagement: 8 weeks, finished 2023-10.

## Stack

Supabase, React, TypeScript
